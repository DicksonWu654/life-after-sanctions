Method, coverage by chain and the data files

What it adds to earlier work

Earlier studies looked at Ethereum addresses alone (arXiv 2507.11721) or at Bitcoin transaction graphs around designation (arXiv 2409.10031), and the vendor blogs cover single actions. This site catalogues every mapped chain in the historical inventory, includes observed Tron histories, compares issuer blacklists with address-listing dates, measures pre-listing activity, and presents data as of 2026-10-23. Rerun scripts/pipeline.py refresh to update.

How the numbers are made

The list comes from OFAC's Sanctions List Service; designation dates come from OFAC Recent Actions and press releases. Chain history comes from public datasets and explorers, where available. Every number is reproducible with the included scripts. Raw API captures are omitted for size; use scripts/pipeline.py refresh to re-fetch them, then rebuild to replay them. raw_ref values identify sources and requests to re-fetch through the request index and collectors. Per-address explorer rows link to public block explorers. Flow charts show gross first-hop transfers; later paths use the separate hop rule. USD values in results were computed from the price sources in data/prices/manifest.csv at the stated valuation times; the public export includes the manifest and fetch script, with price series omitted. Different units (addresses, entities, designation actions, USD, coins) are never added together.
